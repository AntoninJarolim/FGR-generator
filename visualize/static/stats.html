FGR-DATA-MISMATCH-STATS — span match rates per model

Columns are cumulative — each one also counts every stricter tier: EM (verbatim) ⊆ + normalized (also case / quotes / dashes / whitespace) ⊆ + approximate (also ≤5% character edits). The remainder from “+ approximate” to 100% is not found. Macro aggregation: tier rates are first averaged over the spans of each sample, then averaged over samples — every sample weighs the same. Samples with no extracted spans are excluded from the percentages (counted in “# no spans”).
